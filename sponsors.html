Why it matters

Where your money goes

Sponsorship is vital to the success and sustainability of our club, helping us to provide opportunities for players to train, compete and develop in a safe and supportive environment. Your support contributes to the general running of the club, including equipment for training and matches, safeguarding and first-aid training for volunteers as required by England Netball, and essential coaching and venue fees.
